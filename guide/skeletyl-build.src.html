  1. Step 0 · done

    Firmware

    • Both flashed and tested.
    • Keep the L / R marks.
    • Reflash: tap RST to GND twice, copy the file to NICENANO.
  2. Step 1

    Screw inserts

    • Skip if all 6 per half are in.
    • 4 outer: ⅔ deep, flip, press flat.
    • 2 inner: all the way, no flip.

    Never above 350 °C.

  3. Step 2

    Diodes

    • Your boards have empty pads: needed.
    • 18 per half: 15 main, 3 thumb.
    • Your side's label (LEFT / RIGHT) faces up.

    Wrong direction = dead key.

  4. Step 3

    Wiring: three straight ribbons

    • Straight across. Square pad to square pad, then the next hole to the next hole. Never cross wires.
    • A (5 wires) and B (6 wires): main flex → adapter. C (4 wires): main flex → thumb flex.
    • Labels on the adapter won't match the flex. That's expected; follow the square pads, not the letters.
    • Wire: the kit ribbon, or thin 28–30 AWG wire. A and B about 75 mm; C: lay both boards in the case, cut with a little slack.
    • Push in from the printed side, solder on the back, trim flush.

    Ribbon is fragile: bend it as little as possible.

  5. Step 4

    Controller on the adapter

    • Chip side down onto the adapter; the printed side faces you.
    • USB-C points to the B− B+ end.
    • Solder the 12 pins along each side. Only the 2 holes in the white box stay empty; the ringed end hole gets pin 009.
    • L in left, R in right. Skip the TRRS jack.
    • Skip the reset button: its UPDATE pads end up under the controller. To flash later, hold Z and tap Q (bootloader key).
    • Before soldering all pins: solder 2, then beep VCC↔GND and RST↔GND. Both must stay silent, or the controller is in backwards.

    Iron 300 °C max.

  6. Step 5

    Battery

    • The adapter B+ pad goes through the ON/OFF jumper, which ends up hidden under the controller. So skip that pad.
    • On/off: put the slide switch in the red battery wire: battery red → middle pin, a side pin → controller B+ (empty corner hole at the USB end).
    • Black → adapter B−.
    • Before the battery: beep controller B+ ↔ B−. It must stay silent.
    • Charging only works with the switch ON.
    • Battery: foam-tape it to the inside of the bottom plate, over the middle keys. Tape over the switch pins below it. The plate must close with no pressure.
    • Quick power test, then unplug until the end.

    Reversed battery = dead controller.

    Step 6

    Test every key, before switches

    • L on USB, R powered.
    • Touch each key's two switch pads with tweezers. The letter above should appear.
    • Dead key: check its diode and ribbon wire.
    • Thumbs also switch layers when held: see your keymap.
  8. Step 7

    Switches

    • Order: thumbs, then top, bottom, middle row.
    • Press PCB to case, push switch in, pins through, PCB flush.
    • Solder 2 pins.

    The case melts if the iron touches it.

  9. Step 8

    Close the case

    • Adapter: 2 screws into the inner inserts.
    • Bottom plate: 4 screws (TX20).
    • 5 rubber feet.
  10. Step 9

    Pair with the Mac

    • Switch both halves on.
    • Mac: Settings → Bluetooth → Skeletyl.
    • R joins L by itself.

Your keymap: all 6 layers

Thumbs: tap for the big label, hold for the layer in orange. Pick a layer to see it.

Small grey label = hold (⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ home-row mods). ▽ = the key you are holding. Dashed = nothing.
